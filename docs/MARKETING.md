## Problems We Solve

### 1. The Build-or-Outsource Paralysis
**The pain:** "Should we build our own warehouse or use a 3PL?" There's no single right answer — it depends on what your company values most. But without a structured framework, these conversations go in circles for months.

**Our solution:** A 20-criterion weighted scoring tool that forces the leadership team to articulate what matters most (Speed? Cost? Control?) and shows the data-driven answer based on those priorities.

### 2. Hidden Cost Inflection Points
**The pain:** Costs don't scale linearly. A company running 950 orders/month has very different unit economics than one running 1,050 — because that's when you need another full-time employee. These step-function costs blindside growing companies.

**Our solution:** An interactive cost simulator that models fixed, variable, and step-function costs at every volume level. Drag a slider and see exactly where the cost-per-order jumps, when you hit break-even, and what margins look like at scale.

### 3. Board Presentations Built on Vibes
**The pain:** Strategic decisions get presented to boards with PowerPoint slides full of qualitative arguments and cherry-picked data points. No structured methodology. No reproducible analysis.

**Our solution:** Every tool produces shareable URLs, print-ready layouts, and professional visualizations. The board can open the same link, adjust weights to match their own priorities, and see how the conclusion changes. Transparent, reproducible, defensible.

### 4. Decision Fatigue Across Multiple Dimensions
**The pain:** A fulfillment decision involves 20+ competing factors across finance, operations, strategy, quality control, and risk. No human can weigh all of those simultaneously without a structured approach.

**Our solution:** Weighted scoring across categorized criteria with preset scenarios that represent common strategic postures (growth-focused, cost-focused, control-focused, risk-averse). The math handles the complexity so humans can focus on setting priorities.

## Why Senior Leaders Embrace This

### 1. Speed to Insight
Drag a slider, get an answer. No waiting for an analyst to rebuild a model. A 30-minute board discussion can explore five scenarios on the spot.

### 2. Forces Strategic Alignment
The weighted scoring approach forces leadership teams to articulate *what actually matters most* before debating the conclusion. This eliminates circular arguments and surfaces hidden disagreements about priorities.

### 3. Democratizes the Analysis
A CEO, a VP of Ops, and a CFO can each run the tool with their own priorities and compare results. Disagreements become visible, quantified, and productive — instead of political.

### 4. Reduces Decision Risk
These are high-stakes, hard-to-reverse decisions (building a warehouse, signing a 3PL contract, restructuring comp plans). Structured frameworks backed by transparent methodology reduce the chance of expensive mistakes.

### 5. Board-Ready Without Extra Work
The output is the presentation. Print to PDF, share a URL. No reformatting, no slide deck translation layer, no handoff to a designer.

### 6. Removes Consultant Dependency
Companies can run this analysis themselves, as often as they want, with updated assumptions. The value isn't locked in a one-time consulting report that's outdated in six months.
